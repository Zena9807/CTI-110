# The Robot Sandwich

## Assumptions
- The robot is standing in front of a clean kitchen counter with both hands empty.
- Two slices of bread are on a clean plate on the clean counter.
- The sliced ham and sliced cheese are in the refrigerator. 
- Another clean plate and a butter knife are on the counter
- The refrigerator is closed.

## Instructions
1. Walk from the counter over to the refrigerator door.
2. Open the refrigerator door.
3. Locate the sliced ham and sliced cheese.
4. Pick up the sliced ham and sliced cheese.
5. With both the sliced ham and sliced cheese in hand, walk back over to the clean counter.
6. Put the sliced ham and sliced cheese on the clean counter.
7. Walk back to the refrigerator.
8. Close the refrigerator door.
9. Walk back to the clean counter.
10. Pick up the 2 slices of bread.
11. Separate them with one slice of bread on the left side of the plate and one slice of bread on the right side of the clean plate on the counter.
12. Pick up a slice of ham.
13. Put the slice of ham on the slice of bread on the left side of the plate.
14. Pick up a slice of cheese.
15. Put it on top of the ham on the left side of the plate.
16. Pick up the slice of bread on the right side of the plate.
17. Put the slice of bread on top of the cheese on the left side of the plate.
18. You now have a ham and cheese sandwich on the left side of the plate.
19. Move the sandwich to the middle of the plate.
20. Put the sandwich down.
21. Pick up the butter knife on the clean counter by the handle.
22. Use the knife to cut the sandwich down the middle to form 2 equal parts.
23. Put the knife down on the clean counter.

## Done When

Done When: The ham and cheese sandwich is cut into two equal parts, and the knife is put back on the clean counter.
